NEON HARBOR — Streets of Fortune is an original top-down open-world crime game written in plain JavaScript and Canvas, with no libraries and no network requests: just static files. Explore a full city of traffic and pedestrians, steal and drive any car, outrun a five-star wanted system and its police, fight with fists and gunfire, and work through five story missions for fixer Vince — from your first borrowed ride to the City Bank heist. The city lives at night, with lit windows, neon signs, streetlamps and headlights, and every sound effect is synthesized live with WebAudio; progress saves automatically between missions.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
